Forming a New Bond

OLAPLEX is a hair care brand founded in 2014 with the aim of rebuilding broken hair disulfide bonds. This project was developed as a repositioning and communications strategy for OLAPLEX at a time when the brand needed to strengthen credibility, clarify its value proposition, and reconnect with both beauty consumers and professionals. Rather than focusing only on product promotion, the campaign reframed OLAPLEX as a brand that pairs proven bond-building science with human connection, education, and empowerment.

Campaign Management

The Big Idea

Campaign Title:
We’re Bonded by OLAPLEX

Tagline:
“Empowering More Women with Every Bond We Build”

Partner:
“In Partnership with Dress for Success”

The central idea, “We’re Bonded,” turns OLAPLEX’s bond-building technology into a broader emotional platform. The campaign uses the science of repairing hair bonds as a metaphor for rebuilding confidence, trust, and community. This created a concept that was both technically grounded and emotionally resonant.

Target Audience


Primary audience: Gen Z and younger Millennial beauty consumers who value visible results, authenticity, and educational content.

Secondary audience: licensed stylists and salon professionals who influence credibility, product recommendation, and long-term brand trust.

Retail touchpoints: Sephora and Ulta shoppers reached through digital, in-store, and event activations.

Campaign Walk-Through


Pre-launch: teaser content and community sign-up moments introduced the We’re Bonded concept and built early curiosity.

Community launch: a branded digital hub gave consumers and stylists a place to engage with campaign content, product education, and shared stories.

Influencer and stylist PR boxes: curated mailers with QR-enabled educational content generated social buzz while also reinforcing professional credibility.

Hero storytelling: a launch video centered on personal transformation and confidence, linking OLAPLEX’s repair benefits to a larger emotional narrative.

Giveaway: launched through influencer partnerships and organic media posting, we launch a viral #werebonded movement where people share their “Bonded Stories” and in return they get entered in the giveaway

Educational content: short-form social videos drove attention, while still imagery and long-form tutorials explained product usage and OLAPLEX’s numbering system.

Retail and media activation: Sephora and Ulta pop-ups, endcap displays, banner ads, print placements, and out-of-home executions moved audiences from awareness to consideration and purchase.

Engagement and loyalty: the #WereBonded campaign and giveaway encouraged user-generated content, community participation, and post-launch momentum.

Campaign Management

My Role

I served as the Campaign & Marketing lead on the project. My role focused on shaping the campaign strategy, positioning, messaging direction, channel planning, and the overall customer journey from pre-launch through post-launch. I also helped organize how the creative and strategic elements came together into a cohesive portfolio-ready campaign.

Conclusion

Reinforcing Reliability and Brand Heritage

In summary, our campaign aims to build trust and transform young professionals into brand advocates by deepening their understanding of OLAPLEX’s scientific foundation and authentic story. Through transparent communication and modern storytelling, we will highlight how OLAPLEX’s patented bond-building technology delivers real, visible results—earning the trust of a generation that values proof over promises. We will educate and engage Gen Z and younger millennial consumers through approachable, science-backed messaging across digital, social, and experiential platforms. By breaking down the complex chemistry behind the brand in a relatable way, we can bridge the gap between professional credibility and everyday accessibility.
